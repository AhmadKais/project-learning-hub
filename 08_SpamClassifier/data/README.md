# SpamClassifier data

`sms_spam.csv` — the SMS Spam Collection (5,574 messages, 747 spam), converted to `text,label` (the format
your project's `data/corpus/messages.csv` expects):
T. A. Almeida, J. M. Gómez Hidalgo and A. Yamakami, *Contributions to the study of SMS spam filtering: new
collection and results*, ACM DocEng 2011. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CC84
Licence: CC BY 4.0. Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/228/sms+spam+collection
