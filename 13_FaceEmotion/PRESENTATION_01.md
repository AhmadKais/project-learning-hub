# Talk for next week - one algorithm from your project

**Student:** Ryan Raed  ·  **When:** next week's class  ·  **Length:** 10 minutes + questions

Choose **one** algorithm from the list below and teach it to the class. Send your choice to the teacher
privately by the end of this week.

## Choose one

### 1. Convolutional networks (CNN)

- Read: [lesson 22](../lessons/22_convolution_pooling/LESSON.md) and [`05_cnn.md`](01_theory/05_cnn.md)
- An idea for the board: a 4x4 image and a 3x3 edge filter - compute the output, then ReLU and max pooling

### 2. Backpropagation and optimizers

- Read: [lesson 21](../lessons/21_backprop_optimizers/LESSON.md) and [`03_images_nn.md`](01_theory/03_images_nn.md), [`06_data_training.md`](01_theory/06_data_training.md)
- An idea for the board: one neuron, one example - the loss, the gradient, and one step of gradient descent with real numbers

### 3. Face detection with Haar cascades

- Read: [lesson 25](../lessons/25_face_detection_haar/LESSON.md) and [`07_detection_realtime.md`](01_theory/07_detection_realtime.md)
- An idea for the board: a small grid of numbers - build the integral image and get a rectangle's sum with 4 look-ups

## What the talk must show

1. **The problem** the algorithm solves, with an everyday example
2. **How it works**, step by step - in words and pictures, no code
3. **A small example with numbers, worked on the board** (make up your own numbers or use the idea above)
4. **Where it is in your project**: which file and class, and what it gives the app
5. **One limit**: when does it work badly, or what does it get wrong?

## Rules

- 6-8 slides, with drawings and examples - not paragraphs of text.
- Say it in your own words. If you read from the slide, you don't know it yet.
- Practise once at home with a timer, including the board example.
- After your talk the class and the teacher ask questions - be ready to redo one step of your example.
