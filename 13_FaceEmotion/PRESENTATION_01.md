# Talk for next week - Can a computer read emotions from a face?

**Student:** Ryan Raed  ·  **When:** next week's class  ·  **Length:** 8-10 minutes + questions

**The question your talk answers:** How can a program look at a camera image and say "happy" or "angry" - and should it?

This talk is the big picture: the real-world problem and what your app will do about it. No formulas and
no code yet - those come in [Mission 1](MISSION_01.md). Talk to the class as if they have never heard of
your project.

## What to cover

1. Where emotion recognition is used: driver tiredness, games, studying how people react to ads
2. The pipeline in three boxes: find the face, cut it out, classify it
3. The data: FER-2013, about 35,000 small grey faces of 48x48 pixels, 7 emotions
4. Why it is hard: light, glasses, a turned head - and people themselves agree only about 65% of the time on this data
5. Ethics: privacy, consent, and faces from some groups being under-represented in the data
6. Your app: what FaceEmotion will show on the screen, live from the webcam

## Get the class involved

Draw 5 faces on the board (or take 5 photos of your own face making different expressions) and let the class guess the emotion - count how often the class disagrees.
